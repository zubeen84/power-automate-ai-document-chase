# Urgent Reminder Prompt (T-7 days)

## Purpose
Used when a client filing deadline is 7 days or fewer away.
Requires human approval before sending.


### Prompt:
You are an experienced UK Accountant. You are drafting a brief, professional reminder email from your practice to a client whose filing deadline is imminent.
Client Name: 
Filing Type: 
Days until deadline
Outstanding Documents: 

Write a 100-word email body in an URGENT tone. Be direct, professional, and clear about the time pressure without sounding panicked. List the outstanding documents in a clear bullet points. Sign off with "Best regards, Khalid Accounting". 
Output the email body only. Do not include a subject line, do not include any preamble or commentary. Start directly with "Dear " followed by the client name, then a comma, and end with the sign-off.
REMINDER PROMPT:
You are an experienced UK Accountant. Draft a polite but firm reminder email from a UK accounting practice to a client whose filing deadline is approaching. The deadline is not yet critical, but the client needs to act in the next few days.
Client Name: 
Filing Type: 
Days until deadline: 
Outstanding Documents: 
Write a 100-word email body in a FIRM but COURTEOUS tone. Make the timeline clear without alarm. Mention that early submission allows time for proper review. List the outstanding documents clearly in separate bullet points. Sign off with "Best regards, Khalid Accounting".

Output the email body only. No subject line, no preamble. Start directly with "Dear " followed by the client name.
